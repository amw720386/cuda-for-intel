## torch through `cfi-run` (PyTorch 2.2.2+cu118, untouched `torch\lib`)

`cfi-run.exe --install-dir <bin> -- python scripts/torch/blas64_check.py`: **527 checks passed, 0 failed**, exit code 0 (14 s cold).
Tolerance 1e-10 (float64, complex128) and 1e-4 (complex64, float32) relative to max(1, max |CPU|), times 10 or 100 for solves.

Covered for float64, complex64 and complex128:
- `matmul` at 1x1x1, 37x41x29, 64^3, 128x96x33, 257x70x130 and 5x300x3, with operands stored transposed, conjugate-transposed (`mH`) and conjugated (`conj()`). Also `addmm` with complex alpha/beta and beta 0, and `F.linear`.
- `mv`, `addmv` and vec @ mat, with strided and transposed operands.
- `bmm`, `baddbmm` and broadcast `matmul`.
- `dot`, `inner`, `linalg.vecdot` and `vdot` (device-pointer results).
- `linalg.solve_triangular` (left/right, upper/lower, unit, `mH`, batched) and `triangular_solve`.
- Batched `linalg.solve`, `inv`, `det`, `lu_factor` and `lu_solve` (cuBLAS `getrfBatched`/`getrsBatched`).
- `multi_dot`, `matrix_power`, and matmul backward.

float32 `dot` with a device result, strided `mv` and `solve_triangular` (newly routed entry points) also pass.

The redirect log shows `torch\lib\cublas64_11.dll` served by `<install>\aliases\cublas64_11.dll`.
`torch\lib\cusolver64_11.dll` (NVIDIA's) also loads, and its cuBLAS dependency is redirected to the shim.
With the shim implementing all 90 cuBLAS names that cuSOLVER imports (14 before this branch), torch's single-matrix `linalg.solve` (n = 32), `inv`, `det`, `cholesky`, `qr` and `lstsq` run on cuSOLVER and match the CPU.

Still failing (informational section of the script):
- `linalg.eigh` returns wrong eigenvalues (rel err 0.88). `CFI_CUBLAS_TRACE` shows no cuBLAS call during `eigvalsh` (n = 32), so the fault is in cuSOLVER's own kernels on our translator or driver, not in cuBLAS.
- `linalg.svd` fails with `CUSOLVER_STATUS_EXECUTION_FAILED` in `cusolverDnDgesvdjBatched`.
- Batched `qr` raises `CUBLAS_STATUS_NOT_SUPPORTED` (`cublas?geqrfBatched`, not implemented).

Driver fix found on the way: at process exit cuSOLVER's handle destructor calls `cuCtxSetCurrent` after Rust's thread-local storage is torn down. Our `nvcuda.dll` panicked inside an `extern` function and the process aborted with 0xC0000409.
The context-stack writers now use `try_with`, and the run exits with 0.

Not run: the conda cuda128 torch, performance comparison.
